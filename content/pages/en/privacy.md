## 1. Who is the data controller?

- Email: [kontakt@bitspire.pl](mailto:kontakt@bitspire.pl)

## 2. What data do we collect and why?

We collect data only to the extent necessary to:

- handle inquiries submitted through the contact form,
- conduct correspondence related to our offer,
- pursue or defend against claims if necessary.

The processed data includes primarily: **name, email address, phone number (if provided), message content**, as well as technical data such as **IP address and browser identifier** (stored in server logs).

The legal basis for processing is **Article 6(1)(f) of the GDPR** — our legitimate interest in handling user inquiries.

## 3. Cookies

The website uses only **technical cookies** necessary for its proper functioning, including:

- remembering the selected language,
- remembering the theme preference (light / dark).

These cookies do not require consent under cookie regulations, but we inform you about their use.

## 4. Who do we share data with?

Your data may be transferred to the following entities:

- **Vercel, Inc.** — hosting infrastructure provider (server logs),
- **Seohost** — hosting / domain provider,
- **Resend, Inc.** — email service provider (if used for correspondence),
- **Google Search Console** — website ownership verification tool, does not process users' personal data,
- entities authorized under applicable law.

Data is not sold or shared for marketing purposes.

## 5. Transfers outside the EEA

Vercel, Inc. provides services in the United States. Data transfers are carried out with appropriate safeguards in place, including the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses (SCC).

## 6. How long do we keep data?

Data from the contact form is stored for the time necessary to respond and for any subsequent correspondence, **but no longer than 3 years from the date of the last message**. After this period, data is deleted or anonymized, unless longer retention is required by law.

## 7. Your rights

You have the right to:

- access your data,
- rectify your data,
- erase your data ("right to be forgotten"),
- restrict processing,
- object to processing,
- data portability,
- withdraw consent (where processing is based on consent),
- lodge a complaint with the relevant supervisory authority.

All requests can be sent to: [kontakt@bitspire.pl](mailto:kontakt@bitspire.pl).

## 8. Data security

We apply technical and organizational measures appropriate to the risks, including encrypted connections (HTTPS), limited access to data, and the use of trusted service providers.

## 9. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated. Any material changes will be communicated by posting the new version on this page together with the last updated date.
