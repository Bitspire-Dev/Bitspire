## Websites in Ustka — seasonal sales without commissions

Ustka lives off tourism — guesthouses, restaurants, attractions and seasonal services. The key problem for local businesses is dependence on OTA portals and their commissions, reaching up to 20%. Your own fast website is a way to reclaim some bookings and direct contacts — especially from returning guests and customers who come back every year.

### A site that works during the season

For an accommodation property the most important things are: photos that sell the rooms; an always up-to-date price list; and a simple enquiry form or booking calendar. We build it so the owner can change a price or availability themselves — there is no time to wait for a developer before the season. Sites load instantly, because in July nobody waits five seconds for a slow WordPress site on beach Wi-Fi.

### Local SEO in a tourist town

Competition in Ustka is not only local businesses but also large portals. That is why we optimise for concrete phrases (“accommodation with sea view Ustka”, “restaurant by the port”) and take care of the Google Business Profile — often it decides where a tourist calls first. Website, profile and maps must play together.
