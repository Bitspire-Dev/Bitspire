## Websites in Słupsk — local market, measurable results

Słupsk is a city of around 80,000 people where more and more customers search for services on Google rather than in local classifieds. For a regional company, a good website is no longer a formality — it is a real source of enquiries, provided it is fast, technically sound and answers customer questions before the first phone call.

We have proven this in practice. The skladamy.com website, which we designed and built for a furniture company near Słupsk, now generates over 970 clicks and around 42,000 impressions in Google Search Console every month — with no paid campaigns, purely from organic traffic. For a city of this size, that shows local SEO simply works when the site is built properly from the start.

### How we approach a project “for Słupsk”

Every project starts with a conversation about how your customers actually search for services — often phrases like “custom furniture Słupsk” or “hairdresser Słupsk price list”. Based on that, we design the page structure, headings and content first, and only then write the code. SEO is not bolted on at the end — it is embedded in the foundations of the site, from information architecture to loading times on weaker phones.

We build all sites on a modern stack (Next.js), which translates into Core Web Vitals scores that a templated WordPress rarely achieves. On top of that, you get a simple CMS — you can change texts, photos, price lists or news yourself, without scheduling every small change with a developer.
